# Social Launch Draft

These are low-hype launch blurbs for sharing the first public version of the
starter kit. Adapt wording to the platform and audience.

## LinkedIn Short

I put together a free Practical Home Lab Starter Kit for Network Engineers.

It focuses on a secure, repeatable Linux-based lab workflow with GNS3, Ansible,
SSH hardening, UFW, sanitized templates, diagrams, and validation checks.

The goal is simple: help network engineers build a lab they can document,
rebuild, and safely share without exposing real environment details.

## LinkedIn Longer

I published a first version of a Practical Home Lab Starter Kit for Network
Engineers.

The repo is built around a small, repeatable Linux-based lab: GNS3 for virtual
network devices, Ansible for read-only validation, SSH and UFW for safer access,
and Markdown/Mermaid docs for architecture and workflow notes.

It includes sanitized examples only. No real credentials, private keys, tokens,
account data, public-facing addresses, or private environment details belong in
the repo.

This is a v0.1 foundation, not a production design. The intent is to make the
first lab pass more organized, secure, and explainable.

## Reddit-Friendly

I made a free starter repo for a Linux-based network engineering home lab.

It covers a practical GNS3 + Ansible workflow with SSH/UFW hardening, sanitized
templates, Mermaid diagrams, validation scripts, and release checklists.

It is aimed at keeping a home lab repeatable and safe to document, rather than
just collecting disconnected VMs and notes.

Feedback from network engineers would be useful, especially on what examples or
lab scenarios would help next.
